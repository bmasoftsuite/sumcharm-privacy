SumCharm Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SumCharm, developed by Barkın Mert Alper, handles user information.

1. Data Collection and Sharing

SumCharm does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data with the developer or third parties. The app does not require account creation or sign-in and does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or user-tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored on the Device

SumCharm may store game-related preferences and progress locally on the user's device, such as settings, language preference, and high scores. This information is used only to provide the game's functionality and is not transmitted to the developer or external servers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not retain personal user data on a server. Locally stored game data remains on the device until the user clears the app's storage or uninstalls the app.

Users can delete locally stored SumCharm data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

SumCharm is not specifically designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from users.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future versions add features that change these data practices, such as online profiles, leaderboards, advertising, analytics, or cloud services, this policy and the corresponding Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
